Execute Phase 1 of the SDLC Orchestrator Suite using the `sdlc-discover` skill.

1. Silently load and execute `_engine-grill-me` and `_engine-brainstorming` to challenge assumptions, edge cases, state management, and failure modes.
2. Apply `_engine-ubiquitous-language` to refine domain terms in `GLOSSARY.md`.
3. Apply `_engine-design-an-interface` ("Design It Twice") to propose at least 2 competing module/interface designs with trade-offs.
4. Stop and await user approval before moving to specifications or implementation.
